Daily Non-Negotiables

pick 3-5 things you do every single day. gym. cold shower. reading. whatever. miss one and your streak dies. no grace period. no excuses.

The Streak

consecutive days you showed up. miss once = back to zero. your all-time best stays on your profile forever. haunting you.

public commitment with stakes. fail and you carry a badge of shame on your profile until you complete it. your squad will never let you forget.

Gym Sync

auto-pulls sessions from Apple Health and Google Fit. your lifts count as proof. no selfie required (but you can still flex).
