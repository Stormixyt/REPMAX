## Design Context

### Users
Gen Z / Gen Alpha males (14-20). Self-improvement, discipline culture. Browse on phones, mostly at night. Want to feel part of something elite and serious.

### Brand Personality
Raw. Relentless. Unapologetic. Not a wellness app — a weapon against weakness.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: Dark premium SaaS. Think Vercel, Raycast, Linear — but for discipline, not dev tools.
- **Theme**: Dark. Near-black backgrounds, white text, one pop color: red (#e5484d).
- **Reference**: Vercel.com, Raycast.com — clean borders, polished typography, monochrome with sharp accent.
- **Anti-references**: No corporate SaaS blue, no cute/pastel, no cluttered layouts, no warm/amber tones, no industrial/brutalist.
- **Color**: Pure monochrome (black + white) with red as sole accent. Used sparingly.

### Design Principles
1. **Monochrome discipline** — black, white, red. Nothing else. Restraint IS the design.
2. **Typography-driven** — big headlines, clean body, hierarchy through weight and size.
3. **Premium polish** — subtle borders, precise spacing, everything feels intentional. Vercel-level craft.
4. **Clean not cluttered** — generous whitespace, clear sections, no visual noise.
5. **One accent, used sparingly** — red appears only where it matters. Overuse kills its power.
