What is the best AI workout app for progressive overload? +

(3) You train and log sets — REPMAX adapts your program weekly based on real performance data, adjusting weights up when you hit all reps or implementing deload weeks when needed.

What is progressive overload and why does it matter? +

Progressive overload is the principle of gradually increasing stress on your muscles — through more weight, reps, or sets — to stimulate continuous growth. It's the single most important factor for building muscle and strength. Without tracking it, you plateau. REPMAX automates this by analyzing your workout logs and calculating load increases to ensure you're always progressing.

What training splits does REPMAX support? +

All major splits: Push/Pull/Legs (PPL), Upper/Lower, 5/3/1 Wendler, Full Body, Bro Split, Arnold Split, and custom splits. The AI selects the optimal split based on your training frequency, experience level, and goals, then builds a complete mesocycle with proper exercise selection and periodization.

How is REPMAX different from Fitbod or Jefit? +

Unlike Fitbod or Jefit which generate individual workouts, REPMAX builds entire periodized mesocycles — like a real strength coach. The AI understands progressive overload, deload weeks, auto-regulation (RPE/RIR), and biomechanically intelligent exercise selection. REPMAX also includes built-in social features like group chats, gym invites, and a full AI-powered diet tracker.
