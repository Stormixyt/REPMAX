# REPMAX Marketing Playbook

## Positioning
REPMAX should not market itself as "another fitness app." The sharper angle is:

- AI coach + adaptive programming for lifters who want the app to think with them, not just track sets.
- Social gym app for people who actually train together, with chat, lightning invites, calls, streaks, and accountability.
- Built in public by a young founder, which is a real story and gives the brand personality.

Core message:

`REPMAX builds your plan, adapts it weekly, and keeps your people around you so you actually stick to it.`

## Best Growth Loop
The strongest loop for REPMAX is:

1. Post short-form proof content.
2. Push viewers into Discord.
3. Use Discord to turn curious people into testers.
4. Send testers into the app.
5. Turn their wins, streaks, messages, and reactions into the next wave of content.

That is the best fit because REPMAX is visual, social, and habit-driven.

## Channel Priorities
### 1. TikTok
Best top-of-funnel channel.

- Post 2 to 4 videos a day.
- Focus on real phone-screen proof, not polished ads.
- Show the app adapting workouts, sending invites, calling gym friends, and coaching users.
- Use creator-style talking videos, screen recordings, and gym footage.

TikTok For Business says authentic stories, behind-the-scenes content, comment engagement, vertical video, sound-on content, and short videos all help organic performance. Their guidance also highlights creator-led content and using strong organic posts as paid assets later.

### 2. YouTube Shorts
Best second channel because clips can keep compounding longer than TikTok.

- Repost the best TikToks, but tweak the first line and captions so it is not a lazy copy.
- Keep videos vertical.
- Build around hooks like "I made an AI coach fix my split" or "This app called my gym bro when I skipped."
- Use Shorts to drive people to Discord and the waitlist.

YouTube's official Shorts help docs emphasize vertical creation, mobile-friendly filming, short-form discoverability, and that Shorts recommendations depend on viewer response rather than a specific format advantage. That means the hook and retention matter more than trying to game a "type" of video.

### 3. Instagram Reels
Use this as a repost and social-proof layer.

- Post 1 to 2 Reels a day.
- Carousel idea: "Why most workout apps fail lifters."
- Story idea: daily build updates, polls, feature votes, before/after screenshots.

This is an inference from current short-form platform behavior and your product fit, not a direct quote from a source.

### 4. Discord
Best retention and beta feedback channel.

- Put every bio link into Discord, not directly into a cold signup page.
- Let Discord do the qualifying and hype-building.
- Use channels to collect bug reports, feature requests, progress posts, and call for beta testers.

Discord's official docs recommend Community Onboarding so new members pick relevant roles and channels instead of getting overwhelmed.

## Content Pillars
Use these four pillars only. Do not overcomplicate it.

### 1. Build In Public
- "Built call notifications today."
- "Just made the AI coach smarter."
- "Testing push notifications while skipping homework."

### 2. Proof
- Real workout generation clips.
- Before/after of program adjustments.
- Chat/call/lightning invite flows.
- Streaks, PRs, completed sessions.

### 3. Opinion
- "Why random workout generators suck."
- "Why beginners fail on generic plans."
- "Why gym apps should feel social."

### 4. Community
- User reactions.
- Beta tester wins.
- Progress screenshots.
- "Reply with your split and I'll roast it."

## 30-Day Content System
Post this every week:

- 7 build-in-public clips
- 7 proof/demo clips
- 4 opinion clips
- 3 user/community clips
- 2 founder story clips

That gives you 23 posts a week across TikTok, Shorts, and Reels with remixing.

## Video Hooks To Use
- The problem with workout apps is they never adapt.
- I built an app that calls your gym bro when you skip.
- This AI coach actually knows your split, PRs, and goals.
- Most fitness apps are trackers. This one thinks.
- I am building the social app I wish my gym group had.
- Watch this program adjust after one bad week.
- If your plan never changes, it is already failing you.

## Paid Spend Strategy
Do not spend hard on ads yet.

- First, find 3 to 5 organic videos that naturally pull comments, saves, and signups.
- Then boost only those.
- Start tiny and use creator-style footage, not glossy brand ads.

This lines up with TikTok For Business guidance that strong organic content can be amplified after it proves itself.

## Creator Strategy
Once the product is stable enough:

- Recruit 10 to 20 micro-creators in lifting, self-improvement, and student fitness.
- Pay small flat fees or offer lifetime Pro + affiliate commissions.
- Give them 3 briefs:
  - first impression of REPMAX
  - replacing their notes app with REPMAX
  - using REPMAX with a friend

## CTA Strategy
Use only one CTA per post:

- `Join the REPMAX Discord`
- `Comment "beta" and I'll send the invite`
- `Join the waitlist`

Discord should be the main CTA while the product is still tightening up.
